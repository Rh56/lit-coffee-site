Forged, not brewed

Bethlehem was lit long before we were.

Blast furnaces once lit the sky over this stretch of the Lehigh Valley — a whole city timed to the rhythm of a furnace. Those stacks are a landmark now. But a few blocks away something small still runs hot: a drum turning green beans through fire until they crack, twice, and go dark.

We named the shop for the obvious reason. Not only because the beans catch, but because a room full of people drinking good coffee and eating bread that came out of the oven an hour ago has its own kind of glow.

The bakeshop

Bread, the slow way.

Long ferments, a hot oven, and a baker who shows up before the sun does. Loaves and pastries come out through the morning, in whatever quantity a day of hand mixing allows. When they're gone, they're gone.
