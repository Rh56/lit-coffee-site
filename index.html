Forged, not brewed

Bethlehem built its name on fire
long before we roasted a bean here.

Blast furnaces once lit the sky over this stretch of the Lehigh Valley — the glow of Bethlehem Steel visible for miles, an entire city timed to the rhythm of a furnace. That furnace is a museum now. But three blocks off Main Street, something small still runs hot: a drum spinning green beans through fire until they crack, twice, and turn dark.

We named the shop for the obvious reason. Not just because the beans catch — because a room full of people drinking good coffee and eating bread pulled from the oven an hour ago has its own kind of glow.
